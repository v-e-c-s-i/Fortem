Fortem is an open-source piano for playing and comparing musical tuning systems.

• 88 keys, from A0 to C8, with multi-touch input
• Salamander Grand Piano samples and a pure sine tone
• Equal temperament, Pythagorean tuning, just intonation, quarter-comma meantone, Werckmeister III and Kirnberger III
• Adjustable tonic and A4 reference frequency, with note frequencies and cent deviations
• A keyboard range map with zoom and smooth scrolling
• Pedal sustain and optional upward-swipe note latching
• Performance recording, playback and WAV audio export
• A 30-day trash folder for deleted performances
• Scala .scl import for 12-note octave layouts
• MIDI output through available Android MIDI ports
• Russian and English interface, with adjustable control sizes
• Adaptive layouts for landscape and split-screen use

Internal recordings store played notes and changes to settings; Fortem does not record the microphone. MIDI output uses equal temperament with A4 = 440 Hz; unequal tuning of external MIDI instruments is not supported.

No ads, analytics or account sign-in. The Android app requires no internet or microphone permission. Settings and performances stay on the device; system backups follow the device's settings. WAV export uses a folder chosen through Android's file picker.
