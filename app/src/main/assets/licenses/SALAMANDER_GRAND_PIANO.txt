Changes made for Fortem
-----------------------
Fortem includes only velocity layer v07 (30 notes sampled in minor thirds).
At playback time the nearest recording is resampled for the requested key and
musical tuning. The WAV audio itself is included without lossy recompression.

The audio files remain under CC BY 3.0 and are separate from Fortem's
GPL-3.0-only source code license.
