# Fortem changelog

[Русский](CHANGELOG.md) · **English**

## 1.0.3

First release in this repository: Android 1.0.3 and iOS/iPadOS 1.0.3 (build 19).

- An 88-key A0–C8 keyboard with multi-touch, a range map, zoom and inertial scrolling.
- Salamander Grand Piano and a pure sine-wave sound.
- Six built-in tunings, tonic selection, an A4 reference frequency and Scala import.
- Live retuning, note frequency and cents-offset display.
- A pedal for successive chords and optional upward-swipe note latching.
- Recording of notes and tuning changes, playback, replacement takes and WAV export.
- A 30-day Trash with recording recovery and deletion undo.
- Russian and English interfaces, adjustable control sizes and a symbol reference.
- MIDI output with separate local-sound control.
- Fresh installs use piano, 12-TET and tonic C. Upward-swipe latching is off,
  and recordings and Trash are empty.

See the [user guide](USER_GUIDE.en.md) for controls and platform differences.
